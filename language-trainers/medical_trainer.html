Terminologia·medical latin & greek

Roots are written root/vowel — cardi/o. The letter after the slash is a combining vowel: it links the root to the next element, carries no meaning and no gender, and is dropped before an element starting with a vowel (gastr/o + -itis → gastritis, but gastr/o + -scopy → gastroscopy). Most are o from Greek; Latin roots often take i or e (dent/i, chol/e). Typing the vowel is optional — cardi/o, cardio and cardi all count.
